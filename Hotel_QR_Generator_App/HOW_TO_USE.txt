============================================================
              HOTEL QR CODE GENERATOR APP
============================================================

Requirements:
-------------
- Windows PC with Python 3 installed (https://www.python.org/downloads/)
  * Note: Make sure "Add Python to PATH" is checked during Python installation.

Automatic Setup (No manual setup needed!):
-----------------------------------------
When you double-click "Open QR Generator.bat", it will AUTOMATICALLY:
1. Create a virtual environment (.venv) on your computer.
2. Install the required packages (qrcode and pillow).
3. Open the desktop app!

How to Use:
-----------
1. Double-click "Open QR Generator.bat".
2. Enter your Hotel Name, Floors, and Rooms per floor.
3. The live Vercel Backend URL (https://packcheckqrbg.vercel.app) and Admin Key are pre-filled automatically!
4. Click "Generate QR Codes".

Output Files:
-------------
Generated QR codes are saved in the "qr_codes/<hotel-id>/" folder:
- Individual PNG sticker labels for every room (e.g. room_101.png).
- A printable A4 PDF sheet (print_sheet.pdf) with 12 room stickers per page.
- Manifest files (rooms.json & rooms.csv).

All generated room QR tokens will automatically register with your live backend!
